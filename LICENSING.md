# Licensing and provenance

No project-wide public license has been granted for original Stellar application material. Package metadata records `UNLICENSED`; `private: true` prevents accidental package publication. Neither establishes who owns copyright or verifies the repository's remote visibility. The user intends personal ownership and a company-use license; that arrangement remains to be documented.

The repository retains its Stacki Git ancestry. Historical Stacki source remains governed by its original MIT license. Its notice is preserved in [third-party/stacki/LICENSE](third-party/stacki/LICENSE). Retain that notice with any extracted modules or substantial copied portions. The reference checkout is separate from Stellar's build.

The development harness is incorporated at the user's request from the company's harness repository. Preserve its provenance and record local adaptations; its source does not supply a public license granting unrestricted redistribution. Its inclusion here does not establish personal ownership or resolve the company-use agreement.

Dependencies retain their own licenses. Templates, assets, fonts, generated media and client materials require their own provenance. See [source inventory](docs/provenance/sources.json) and the [ownership discussion](docs/BOOTSTRAP-PLAN.md).
